Our Mission

About Us

Justice League Accessible Design Co. is a fictional digital agency inspired by the teamwork of DC's Justice League. Our mission is to create responsive, readable, and accessible digital experiences.

What We Do

Justice League Services

Each hero represents a different accessibility or web design service.

Superman: Inclusive Content

We organize pages with clear headings, readable text, meaningful alt text, and understandable content.

Batman: Accessibility Audits

We investigate websites for missing labels, weak contrast, confusing navigation, and keyboard accessibility problems.

Wonder Woman: Responsive Design

We use Flexbox, CSS Grid, and media queries to create layouts that work across different screen sizes.

The Flash: Performance & Usability

We organize websites so users can quickly find information and complete important tasks.

Green Lantern: Accessible Interfaces

We design clear links, buttons, menus, and forms with visible keyboard focus states.

Aquaman: Cross-Device Testing

We test websites on mobile, tablet, and desktop screens to keep content readable and usable.
